We're more connected than ever — and somehow further apart.

Life gets full. You think of someone you care about, but you never quite know if it's a good time to call — so you text first, and wait. Or you don't. Weeks slip by. Months. Porch Light was built to change that.

Three simple ways to stay connected

At Home

You're home. It's a good time.

When you walk in the door, a notification asks if you're open to a call. One tap lights your porch light, and it stays on until you head out or turn in for the night. Swipe it away if tonight's not the night. Like flipping on the porch light when you get in.

On the Drive

Miles you'd spend alone anyway.

You're hands-free and going nowhere. One tap lights your light for the drive, and it goes dark when you park. Turn the commute into a warm catch-up. Like waving to a neighbor as you pass their porch.

Scrolling

Trade the feed for a friend.

Open any app you choose and your phone asks How long? Pick your minutes and your light comes on. You set a limit, and a friend gets the chance to catch you. It goes dark when you're done. Android now, iPhone coming. Like stepping outside instead of staring at the TV.

See a light on? Ring the Doorbell.

Tap a friend whose light is on and their Doorbell rings. If they answer, you call. If they don't, it disappears after five minutes without a trace. No missed call left sitting on their phone.

No posts. No likes. No strangers.

Porch Light isn't social media. There's no feed to perform for, no audience, no followers to chase. Just genuine, one-on-one conversation with the handful of people who actually matter to you.
