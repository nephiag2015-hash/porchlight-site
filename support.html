Common Questions

How do I let people know I'm free to talk?
Turn on your Porch Light from the Home screen. The people in your circles will see it and know you're reachable.

How is this different from a normal call?
There's no scheduling or texting first — your light does the asking. You can also turn Auto Porch Light on so it switches on by itself when you get home.

Can I control who sees my light?
Yes. Only the friends and circles you've added can see your availability. You can block or report anyone directly from the app.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your data from our systems.

Still Stuck?

Email support@theporchlightapp.com with your device type and a quick description of what happened, and we'll get back to you.
